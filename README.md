# Adidas-Profitability-Report
# 📊 Retail Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboards

This repository contains three interactive dashboards designed to analyze retail performance across different dimensions including **retailers, sales channels, products, and quarterly performance**.

The dashboards provide clear business insights into profitability trends, sales distribution, and performance comparisons to support data-driven decision making.

---

## 📌 Dashboard 1: Retailer Performance Overview

### 🔎 Description
This dashboard presents the **total profit generated by each retailer** using a horizontal bar chart.  
It helps identify the highest and lowest performing retailers based on total profit.

### 📈 Visualization
- Horizontal Bar Chart
- Metric: **Total Profit**
- Dimension: **Retailer Name**

### 🏪 Retailers Included
- Walmart
- Target
- Best Buy
- Costco
- Sam’s Club
- Amazon

### 💡 Key Insights
- Walmart and Costco generate the highest total profit among all retailers.
- Sam’s Club and Amazon show comparatively moderate performance.
- The visualization allows quick benchmarking between retailers.

### 🎯 Business Value
- Identify top-performing retail partners.
- Support strategic decisions for partnerships and expansion.
- Detect underperforming retailers for improvement plans.

## 📌 Dashboard 2: Sales Channel Distribution & Quarterly Trends

This dashboard combines two visualizations to analyze sales structure and time-based trends.

---

### 🥧 Part A: Sales Channel Distribution

#### 🔎 Description
A pie chart displaying the percentage contribution of each sales channel to total sales.

#### 📊 Sales Channels
- In-Store → 15%
- Online → 56%
- Outlet → 29%

#### 💡 Key Insights
- Online sales dominate overall performance (more than half of total sales).
- Outlet contributes nearly one-third of total sales.
- In-store sales represent the smallest share.

#### 🎯 Business Value
- Emphasizes the importance of digital transformation.
- Supports investment decisions in e-commerce platforms.
- Helps optimize channel-specific marketing strategies.

---

### 📈 Part B: Quarterly Sales Trend

#### 🔎 Description
A line chart comparing performance across four quarters (Q1–Q4).

#### 📊 Observations
- Peak performance occurs in Q2.
- Gradual decline from Q3 to Q4.
- Clear seasonal trend pattern.

#### 💡 Key Insights
- Q2 represents the strongest sales period.
- Q4 shows the lowest performance.
- Indicates potential seasonality or demand fluctuations.

#### 🎯 Business Value
- Helps forecast demand.
- Assists in inventory planning.
- Supports quarterly budgeting and marketing campaigns.

---

## 📌 Dashboard 3: Product Profitability Analysis

### 🔎 Description
This dashboard analyzes total profit generated by each product category using a vertical bar chart.

### 📦 Product Categories
- Men's Street Footwear
- Men's Athletic Footwear
- Women's Street Footwear
- Women's Athletic Footwear
- Men's Apparel
- Women's Apparel

### 📈 Visualization
- Vertical Bar Chart
- Metric: Total Profit
- Dimension: Product Category

### 💡 Key Insights
- Women's Athletic Footwear generates the highest profit.
- Men's Athletic Footwear follows as the second strongest category.
- Apparel categories show moderate performance.
- Men's Street Footwear generates the lowest profit among footwear categories.

### 🎯 Business Value
- Identifies most profitable product segments.
- Supports merchandising and stock planning.
- Guides promotional strategies by category.
